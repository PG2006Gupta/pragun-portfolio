01About

Curiosity in.
Something real out.

I turn stubborn questions into working interfaces, useful experiments and visuals that make people stop scrolling — then keep refining until the idea feels inevitable.

I'm Pragun Gupta — a developer, vibe coder and Information Technology student pursuing a Bachelor of Engineering in Information Technology at Chandigarh University, Punjab, India. I’m working toward 2029, with plenty more building and learning ahead. Give me a stubborn question and a blank canvas, and I’ll turn the idea into something you can click, test and remember.

My playground is where code meets communication: prompt engineering to make AI more useful, poster designing to make a message impossible to ignore, and full-stack builds to make ideas actually work. From Shop Zone and FormShield to City Care Hospital and Smart Attendance Portal, I learn by shipping, breaking and rebuilding.

I care about the moment a project stops feeling like a demo — when the flow feels natural, the copy earns its place and the interface quietly does its job. I’m still building the journey, but every project is another chance to make the next one sharper.
